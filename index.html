Meet the owner

Tshepo Motshoni

The person behind Medico-Legal & Forensic Services — and the reason every case gets the precision it deserves.

I'm Tshepo — founder of Medico-Legal & Forensic Services, a practice built on one simple idea: every case deserves the same precision, integrity, and respect as if it were our own.

I started this practice because I saw too many cases handled with haste and carelessness. Every document, every evidence chain, every detail — it all matters. I built a team that treats each case the way I'd treat my own.

From Johannesburg and beyond, we've delivered over 500 cases with a reputation for precision, integrity, and discretion. Whether it's a complex forensic review or an urgent legal-medical consultation, we show up on time, handle the evidence properly, and deliver results you can trust.

Beyond the practice, I'm passionate about building things — stronger legal systems, safer communities, and opportunities for South Africans. Medico-Legal & Forensic Services is part of that bigger picture.
