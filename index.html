About

Tshepo Motshoni

The person behind Medico-Legal & Forensic Services — and the reason every case gets the precision it deserves.

I'm Tshepo Motshoni — founder of Medico-Legal & Forensic Services, a Johannesburg-based practice built on one principle: every case deserves the same precision, integrity, and respect as if it were our own.

I started this practice because I saw too many medico-legal matters handled with haste and carelessness. Every document, every evidence chain, every clinical detail — it all matters. I built a team that treats each case the way I'd treat my own family's case.

With over 150 cases handled and a 98% opinion acceptance rate in court, we've earned a reputation for delivering opinions that stand up under cross-examination. Whether it's a complex forensic document review or an urgent legal-medical consultation, we show up on time, handle the evidence properly, and deliver results you can trust.

Beyond the practice, I'm a South African builder — of stronger legal systems, safer communities, and opportunities for people who've been overlooked. Medico-Legal & Forensic Services is part of that bigger picture.
